# Last Call — Narrative Script

**Setting:** A small American town, autumn of 1960. Night shift at the local telephone exchange. You — *the operator* — are working your final shift before the new automated system replaces you tomorrow morning. The town's switchboard has six lit sockets, each labeled with a destination.

**Theme hook:** "Connections" — literal cables, but also the human ones we make and lose. The game is about being the invisible thread between people, one last time.

**Run length:** 10 calls, ~8–10 minutes of play.

---

## Cast

- **You** — the operator. Never seen, never speaks. The player.
- **Daniel Hayes** — early 30s, lives two hours away on Highway 40. Calling in throughout the night about his mother.
- **Margaret Hayes ("Mrs. Hayes")** — Daniel's mother. 7th & Vine. Never answers.
- **Doc Wheeler** — town doctor. Knows more than he's letting on.
- **Reverend Carter** — runs the chapel. Becomes Daniel's eyes on the ground.
- **Sheriff Briggs** — sheriff's office. Calm, methodical.
- **Patty** — owns Patty's Diner. Knows everyone, sees everything.
- **Cole** — runs Cole's Garage. Gossip relay.
- **Mrs. Henley** — nosy neighbor on 8th street.

## The six sockets (recipients)

1. **Mrs. Hayes** — 7th & Vine
2. **Doc Wheeler** — Clinic
3. **Sheriff Briggs** — Sheriff's Office
4. **Reverend Carter** — Chapel
5. **Patty's Diner**
6. **Cole's Garage**

(A 7th socket — **Highway 40 Truck Stop, Marker 88** — lights up only for call 9. A final socket — **County General Hospital** — lights up only for call 10. This keeps the layout clean while letting the endgame *open up*.)

---

## The 10 calls

Each entry is:
**Caller** → *correct socket*
> Caller's request (what the player hears/reads on the screen).

After the player connects correctly, a short post-connect line plays, then the screen fades to the next call.

---

### Call 1 — *Tutorial. Easy. Teaches the verb.*
**Patty** → Cole's Garage
> "Mornin', operator. Patty here. My delivery truck won't crank — could you put me through to Cole's Garage?"

*Post-connect:* (Cole picks up. "Cole's. Yep, I'll be over in twenty.") Click.

---

### Call 2 — *The hook.*
**Daniel Hayes** → Mrs. Hayes (7th & Vine)
> "Hello? Operator? I need to reach Mrs. Hayes on 7th and Vine. She's my mother — she didn't answer her morning call. I'm two hours out on Highway 40."

*Post-connect:* (Ring. Ring. Ring. No answer.) Daniel: "She… she must've gone to the market. I'll try again later. Thanks."

---

### Call 3 — *First drop of dread.*
**Mrs. Henley** → Sheriff Briggs
> "Operator, this is Henley over on 8th. There's been an ambulance sitting two streets over near Vine for ten minutes. Connect me to the sheriff, would you? Something's not right."

*Post-connect:* Sheriff: "Appreciate the call, Mrs. Henley. We're already on it. Stay inside."

---

### Call 4 — *Filler. Establishes Doc + Reverend exist.*
**Reverend Carter** → Doc Wheeler
> "Operator — the Chapel here. I've got old Mr. Bray taking a turn during evening prayer. Put me through to Doc Wheeler, please."

*Post-connect:* Doc: "On my way, Reverend."

---

### Call 5 — *Daniel comes back. Story tightens.*
**Daniel Hayes** → Doc Wheeler
> "Operator, it's Daniel Hayes again. Still no answer at Mom's. Could you try Doc Wheeler? She had a check-up Thursday — maybe he knows if she went somewhere."

*Post-connect:* Doc, quietly: "Daniel… your mother came in Thursday with chest pains. I told her to call me at the first sign it came back. Son — drive faster."

---

### Call 6 — *Outside confirmation. Player now knows.*
**Sheriff Briggs** → Patty's Diner
> "Patty? Briggs. Has Margaret Hayes been in today? Henley says there's a unit at her place."

*Post-connect:* Patty: "She came in yesterday morning. Said she was tired. Hasn't been in today, Sheriff. Oh god."

---

### Call 7 — *Daniel rallies the ground team.*
**Daniel Hayes** → Reverend Carter
> "Operator — the Chapel, Reverend Carter. Hurry, please."

*Post-connect:* Reverend: "I'm leaving now, son. I'll be at your mother's door in five. Stay on the road."

---

### Call 8 — *The hint that lands.*
**Cole** → Patty's Diner
> "Patty, it's Cole. Truck stalled out near 7th and Vine — there's two units and a chaplain at Margaret's place. Anyone heard from Daniel?"

*Post-connect:* Patty: "He's on Highway 40. Lord, he doesn't know yet."

---

### Call 9 — *The pivotal connection. New socket lights up.*
**Reverend Carter** → Highway 40 Truck Stop, Marker 88
> "Operator. I need to reach Daniel Hayes — he's on the highway. Try the truck stop payphone at marker 88. Tell him his mother's alive. Tell him they're taking her to County General."

*Post-connect:* Static. A trucker grabs the phone, runs out, flags Daniel down. Daniel's voice, distant, breathless: "Tell the Reverend — tell him I'm coming."

*This is the call the whole game pivots on. Mis-route this and the bad ending triggers immediately.*

---

### Call 10 — *The last call.*
**Daniel Hayes** → County General Hospital
> "Operator… please. County General. I'm in the parking lot. I need to know if I can still — "

*Post-connect (good ending):* The line connects. We hear a nurse: "Mr. Hayes? She's awake. She's been asking for you." Daniel's breath catches. The line goes warm with quiet. The operator's board goes dark, one light at a time.

*Final on-screen text:* "*Shift ended, 03:47. Margaret Hayes was discharged six days later. The new automated exchange went live at sunrise.*"

---

## Endings

### Good ending — *Connected*
- Triggered if call 10 is routed correctly **and** the player still has at least 1 patience token.
- Scene as above.

### Bad ending — *Static*
- Triggered if patience tokens reach 0 (3 wrong connections) **or** call 9 is mis-routed.
- The board goes dark mid-call. Daniel's voice, alone: "Mom? … Mom?" Static. Cut to black.
- *Final on-screen text:* "*Some calls don't get through. The operator clocked out at 03:47.*"

---

## Why this works for the jam scoring rubric

- **Theme (high):** every mechanic *is* connection. Cables, people, a son to his mother.
- **Story (high):** rare in jams; six characters with names + voices in <800 words of dialogue.
- **Mechanics (medium):** one verb (drag cable to socket), reused with rising stakes.
- **Aesthetics (high, cheap):** sepia + CRT + AI portraits — small surface area to polish.
- **Sound (high, cheap):** dial tones, ringing, static, plug-clicks do 80% of the atmosphere.
- **Music (medium):** one melancholic CC-BY loop is enough.
- **Fun (medium):** short, tense, replayable for the second ending.

---

## Writing rules for tweaks

- Keep each caller request to ≤ 2 sentences. Read aloud — if you stumble, cut it.
- Never name the correct socket in the request. The player should map *people/places* to sockets themselves, that's the puzzle.
- Filler calls (3, 4, 6, 8) exist to drop hints. Every line is a clue.
