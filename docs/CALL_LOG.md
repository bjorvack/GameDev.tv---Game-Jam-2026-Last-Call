# Briar Hollow Telephone Exchange — Operator's Log

```
EXCHANGE:    Briar Hollow (BHE-02)
SHIFT:       Friday, 11 November 1960, 20:00 – 04:00
OPERATOR:    M. ████  (initials redacted, final shift)
BOARD:       6 standard + 2 contingency sockets
PATIENCE:    3 standard tokens at shift start
WEATHER:     Clear, 46°F, wind WSW 5 mph
```

> **Codes used in this log:**
> `CONN` – call connected & completed normally
> `NA`   – ringback, no answer
> `MR`   – misroute (operator error or caller correction)
> `TO`   – time out, caller hung up before connect
> `SC`   – contingency socket lit (non-standard line)
> `△`    – note in operator's hand in right margin
